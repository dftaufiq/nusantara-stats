Nusantara Stats — a national statistics site for Indonesia, 2015–2024: population, economy, prices & work, health & education, provinces, and a full data explorer. Every figure carries its source and year. All national figures verified against the World Bank World Development Indicators; province figures from BPS 2024 and Dukcapil Kemendagri. Static files, no build step, no network requests, hand-drawn SVG charts, vanilla JS.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — Beranda, Penduduk, Ekonomi, Harga & Kerja, Kesehatan & Pendidikan, Daerah, Jelajahi Data, Tentang & Sumber. Try the year selector on Jelajahi Data, the province search + column sort on Daerah, and the metric toggle on Penduduk.
